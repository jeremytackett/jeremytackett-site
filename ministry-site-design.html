services — ministry site design

A Straightforward Website Package for Church Leaders and Individuals in the Non-Profit Space

Ministry is a calling of passion, and most of us aren't getting rich doing it. My goal is to help you tell your story, expand the reach of your work, and create a bit of side-hustle income for me, too. (We can keep it real, since we're friends.) As I've worked with ministry partners to build their sites, we've been creative with ways to keep costs down using free web services and sticking to Squarespace for domain purchasing and web hosting.

what it costs

Here's how it breaks down

$0 — Introductory consultation.
This whole process is built on relationship. To make sure we're a good fit and that you're comfortable with the process and the platform, we start out with a free 10-15 minute call. This time gives us a chance to meet, and gives you the chance to see how a site is built and easily updated.

$550 — One-time fee for our collaboration and site creation.
This fee covers all the steps. I'll take you start-to-finish through the process of creating your ministry site, including our introductory conversation, helping you collect and develop your content, and teaching you how to update/change your finished site as often as you'd like. Discounts are available for seminary students, members of Young Clergy Women International, and others. Get in touch for more information.

This is a fee you pay directly to Squarespace, and it's paid every year (or every month if you choose). This covers the web hosting for your site. I'll help you get this set up if you'd like. Squarespace takes care of hosting and domain purchasing, so you don't have to worry about renewing and connecting a domain to your site. the process

How It Works

Step Zero

A chance to look before you leap.

This model may be a good fit for lots of situations, but before you jump in I want to make sure it's a good fit for you. The best way I've found to do that is to set up a free 10-15 minute Google Hangouts meeting consultation. I'll share my screen with you and walk you through a site I've designed, and let you check out the "back end" so you can see for yourself how easy it is to make updates to your finished site. Once you're ready for a test drive, simply fill out the inquiry form.

Step One

Let's talk.

To create a custom site to meet your needs, the first step is building a relationship with you. That starts with a conversation. Since we're likely in different parts of the world, we'll use Google Hangouts to make that conversation happen.

This 20-30 minute video call gives us the chance to get to know one another and we'll start the process of making your vision a reality.

Step Two

Guided Introspection.

Once we've had the chance to dream together, we'll set up a shared folder on Google Drive or Dropbox, whichever you prefer to use. There, you'll find a document that will guide you through creating content for each page of your site (the package includes up to four separate pages, plus a contact form). You'll be able to title your site sections and write the words you want to share. Any pictures, videos, sermons, or other media that you want to share with the world can simply be dropped into the folder, and I'll take it from there.

Step Three

A custom online home.

As part of the setup process, you'll create an account with Squarespace, and purchase your domain name and hosting plan. I'll guide you through selecting a template, and we'll completely customize the site to fit your needs and personality. Our goal is to create a space that uniquely represents you and your call.

Using the content you've provided in step two, I'll build out a complete, functional site that you can update, add to, remove from, or change in any way you need - without any need for coding knowledge or technical know-how.

Once you've submitted the content for your site, the creation process is typically completed within 1-2 weeks. I'll keep you in the loop all along the way.

Step Four

Giving you the keys.

Throughout the design process, you'll be able to see how the site looks and have input on the creation. When we're ready to launch, we get to do my favorite part - handing you control of your new home.

But you're not alone. Just like when we started the process, we'll wrap up with another Google Hangouts call. Over the course of 30-45 minutes, I'll walk you through your entire site. You'll learn how to make updates, add your own content, and change anything you'd like to keep your story current.

And the best part is, we'll record the conversation and I'll provide you a private download link so you can review any of the instruction at any time.
