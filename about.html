Hey, I'm Jeremy.

My life's journey is a dynamic blend of creativity, purpose, and a hint of adventure that takes me across the country and around the world. I'm based in Philadelphia by way of Eastern Kentucky, and most days you'll find me helping people and organizations figure out how to tell their story well.

the long version

A bit more of the story

I'm dedicated to guiding individuals and teams, helping them unlock their creative potential and envision impactful projects, ministries, and passions. This path is informed and guided by an inquisitive nature, and I thrive on connecting with leaders and creatives in a myriad of settings and contexts.

My journey has led me to rural, community-based work, large-scale corporate endeavors in bustling urban environments, and everything in between. It's been a testament to the transformative power of creativity and collaboration.

While I thrive in the creative pursuits of my career, I cherish moments with my partner Callie and our two incredible kids, not to mention our lively dog Rufus. When the workday winds down, you'll often find me chasing the perfect photograph or planning our next adventure exploring new corners of the world.

One of my proudest achievements was creating the Concert for the Human Family, a musical experience designed to break down barriers and inspire conversations around reconciliation and unity. I'm also deeply passionate about storytelling, as showcased by The Way of Love, a podcast chronicling unique perspectives on life, love, and faith, hosted by Presiding Bishop Michael Curry.

Rooted in purpose and seasoned with diverse experiences, I'm an enthusiast, a family person, a creative guide, and an explorer of the world's wonders. Thanks for joining me on this ever-evolving adventure.
