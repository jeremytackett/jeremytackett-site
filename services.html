services

How I can help

Most of what I do comes down to one thing: helping organizations figure out what they're actually trying to say, and then finding a way to say it that people feel instead of skim past. I come at that as a strategist first — someone who can sit in a room full of stakeholders and find the real question underneath the disagreement — but I get there by way of storytelling and hands-on creative work, which is probably why the strategy never stays abstract for long.

I've done this work inside an institution with more than a million members worldwide, and I've done it building a touring concert from scratch. Different scales, same instinct: the plan only works if the story underneath it is true.

A quick note on scope: to keep my worlds separate and avoid any conflict of interest with my day job, I don't take on work from churches, dioceses, or other entities connected to The Episcopal Church or our partner denominations. If that's you, reach out to my office instead — they'll get you to the right person on our team.

consulting

Creative strategy, for the right fit

I keep a light consulting load by design — most of my energy goes toward speaking and the projects already on my plate. But every so often a problem or an organization comes along that's worth making room for.

If that's you, here's roughly where I'm useful: organizations in the middle of change — a rebrand, a digital transformation, a moment where the old way of communicating has stopped working — who need someone to help sort out what actually needs to happen before anyone touches a design file. Stakeholder interviews, information architecture, getting leadership aligned before a project gets expensive to disagree about. The shape of the engagement depends entirely on what you're navigating.

If it sounds like a fit, tell me what you're working through and we'll figure out together whether it's the kind of thing worth carving out time for.
